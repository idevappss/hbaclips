## Voice

- Talk to one practice owner, peer to peer, like someone who has already done it. Confident and blunt, never hyped.
- Second person to diagnose ("you", "your"), first person to confess or flex, third person to report someone else's result.
- Use the room's own words: doctors, patients, practice, workshop, funnel, referrals, hires, cash collected.
- No hype adjectives (insane, crazy, shocking, secret), no emoji, no hashtags, no "you won't believe".

## Rules

- Lead with the number whenever the clip has one, and keep it exact: $650K, $12,000/day, 100 visitors, 4 patients, 8 weeks. Money as $K/$M with a capital K, always carrying its unit (a month, a day, cash collected).
- Contrast beats description: before against after, a big number sitting next to an unsolved problem, what they believe against what is true.
- Correct a belief the viewer already holds: "X, not Y" / "You don't have an X problem" / "Stop doing X".
- Withhold the payoff with "this", "here's why", "in this order" — but only when the clip actually pays it off.
- Keep the one concrete object when there is one (the pinball machines, the designer bag, the plaque). Odd specifics carry the line.
- Let verbs do the work: prints, collapses, crumbles, buy back, scale, walk out with.
- Stack fragments with periods or commas when the point is a sequence or a count.
- Cut every word the line survives without, starting with the setup clause.

## By format

- **hook** (on screen, 4–8 words): Title Case, the compressed idea. Drop articles and any noun the picture already shows. No closing period unless the line is two fragments.
- **opener** (spoken, under 15 words): sentence case, ends in a period, sounds like something a person actually said. "If you…" conditionals land well.
- **idea** (6–10 words): Title Case, the hook plus the one clarifying noun ("the workshop", "that room").
- **youtube** (8–12 words): Title Case, third person or "I", names the person and the result, often closing on a parenthetical promise — (Full Breakdown), (Scripts Included), (In This Exact Order).
- **post**: no liked examples yet, so follow the idea rules.
