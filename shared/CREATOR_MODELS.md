# Creator models: Dom Iacovone, Brez, Jeremy Haynes

The creator wants every uploaded video turned into shorts that look, read and think like these three accounts, adapted to their niche: **doctors, chiropractors, physical therapists and lifestyle**. This file is the study (what each account actually does, measured where possible) and the spec for replicating it in HBA Clips.

Studied 2026-09-11 by the Dope Clips session. Sources: public metadata for the latest 40 YouTube Shorts per channel (titles, durations, views, likes, dates), the latest 12 Instagram reels' post captions, and frames watched in the browser. No videos were downloaded yet, so colors, sizes and positions are read off screenshots and marked ≈. See "Still to measure".

**Model the structure, never the content.** Reuse formats, pacing, caption treatment and idea patterns. Never reuse their lines, titles, footage or music.

## Dom Iacovone: "CEO vlog lesson"

**Numbers** (40 Shorts, Aug 22 – Sep 10): 2 a day, every day. Half are under 30 s. Best performers: "This Mistake Cost Us $40,000" (30 s, 13.5K), "Taste Testing New Product" (17 s), "Employees That Want To Be CEO's" (55 s), "A Beautiful Thing To Hear as a CEO" (19 s). They're grouped in a series playlist, "Week In The Life of a 9 Figure CEO".

**Look**
- Native vertical, full-bleed. Gym, office, runs, meetings.
- Cinematic grade: desaturated, soft contrast with lifted blacks, cool shadows, warm skin. Closest app look: `moody-film` or `teal-orange` at low strength.
- Shallow depth of field. It looks shot, not screen-recorded.

**Captions**
- White, all lowercase, heavy geometric sans (Montserrat/Proxima ExtraBold style).
- **2 words per caption** (1–3), keeping commas and periods ("assistant," "for two weeks.").
- Centered horizontally at **≈60% of the frame height**, over the chest, not the face.
- Soft drop shadow, no outline, no box, no highlight color. Hard switch per phrase, no pop animation.
- No burned-in headline or hook card.

**Titles**: Title Case, median 6 words, no emoji. Three shapes:
- lesson ("How To Compensate Your Best Employees", "The Boring Sh*t Makes You Rich")
- confession with a number ("This Mistake Cost Us $40,000")
- behind-the-scenes ("Pumped For The New Office Space")

**Idea engine**: real footage from the day plus **one** business or leadership lesson said to camera or as voice-over. One idea per short, no filler. The call to action lives in the description (a mentorship link), not in the video.

## Brez: "story clip"

**Numbers** (40 Shorts, Jun 5 – Jun 16): 3.3 a day. Best: "Holy Richard Mille Mogged😳" (16 s, 711K), "“I have all da colors”🤣🤣" (21 s, 195K), "Brez gets the publics honest opinion on his sweet brand🍬🤔" (38 s, 140K), "Brez Would NEVER Date a OF Girl💀" (18 s, 133K). The longer ones (30 s+) are the exception.

**Look**
- Landscape (16:9) footage **letterboxed** in the middle of a black 9:16 frame: black band on top with the headline, black band on the bottom.
- Natural color, no heavy grade. Restaurant, car, stream and IRL footage.

**Headline** (the hook, visible the whole clip)
- White, bold rounded sans (TikTok Sans / Proxima Soft style), **lowercase conversational**, 5–9 words on 1–2 lines.
- Sits directly on top of the video.
- States the premise so the setup lands in one second: "timothy mogged brez with his $270k richard mille", "brez's take on dating OF girls". Keeps numbers and $ amounts.

**Captions**
- **1–2 words** at a time, lowercase, the same bold rounded sans, centered on the video band (≈50% of the frame).
- **Color rotates per phrase**: white → lime-yellow (≈#E6F56B) → mint (≈#86F2D2).
- Thin dark shadow, no box or outline.

**Titles**
- Third person with a name: "Brez …", "Oblivion Couldn't Believe What Brez Said😳😭".
- Curiosity or reaction framing.
- **One word in CAPS** for stress (NEVER, BIG, ACTUALLY).
- **1–2 emoji at the end** (😳 😭 💀 🥀 🔥 🤑). 35 of 40 titles have emoji.
- Number-only titles ("-$22,000🥀💔") and "X & Y Core." meme titles.

**Idea engine**: moments *with other people*: reactions, a question put to someone, money flexes, relationship takes, asking the public. The clip is the moment, the headline is the setup, and the title is the reaction.

## Jeremy Haynes: "insight reel + comment funnel" (the mentor)

**Numbers**: Instagram, 135K followers; recent reels 1K–9.8K views; several per day. His YouTube (@JeremyHaynesTraining, 42.8K) is long-form: live consults and business breakdowns. The reels look cut from that kind of teaching.

**Look**
- Talking head, medium shot, **dark studio** with an "INNER CIRCLE" neon sign, often holding a tablet.
- Cutaways: whiteboard "Business Breakdown" numbers, simple slide graphics with green icons ("critical thinking").
- Brand color **green** (same green as his YouTube banner).

**On-screen text, two layers**
1. Small white lowercase caption, one word at a time, mid-frame ("closer", "opportunities", "inefficient").
2. A **big keyword punch**: the key word or phrase in bold condensed ALL CAPS, brand green (≈#3DDC5A), dark shadow, spanning the lower middle of the frame. Examples: "IRRATIONAL", "YOURSELF", "DOUBLE CLAIMED", "IMPACT", "FOR YOU". It lands on the word he's stressing.

**Post caption formula** (all 12 recent reels follow it)
1. **Counterintuitive principle** as one punchy sentence: "Charging less to make the yes easier is one of the most expensive habits in business."
2. **Mechanism or proof**: why it's true, or a specific client story with numbers ("One guy sat at a 40% show rate… got to 75%").
3. **Reframe** into a short, quotable truth: "Effort is not the metric. Impact is."
4. **Keyword CTA**: `Comment "SCALE" below and I'll send you a scaling plan…👇`. The keyword matches the topic (SCALE, SHOW, WEBINAR, FACEBOOK, AI).

**Idea engine ("his brain")**: he starts from something concrete and turns it into a business principle.
- An **analogy from elsewhere**: the unexplored part of a video game map, sharks and leftovers.
- A **client case** with a number (a 40% → 75% show rate).
- A **tool** (AI: output is capped by the quality of the question).

Topics rotate through a few buckets: pricing, show rate, funnels/webinars, ad volume, AI, impact vs. effort, positioning. Every reel ends at a resource to comment for.

## Adapted to doctors, chiropractors, PTs and lifestyle

Three presets, one per model. Each takes a normal upload and produces shorts in that model's format. The example lines are original and written for the niche.

### 1. "Practice Vlog" (Dom model)
- **Use for**: day-in-the-life practice footage, training staff, running the clinic, workouts, family and lifestyle.
- **Clip**: 20–35 s, one lesson per short, cut at the end of the lesson sentence.
- **Look**: full-bleed 9:16, moody film grade (low strength), no headline.
- **Captions**: 2 words, white, lowercase, heavy sans, center at 60%, soft shadow, no highlight.
- **Title**: Title Case lesson or number confession.
  - "The Front Desk Mistake That Cost Us $12,000"
  - "How I Train New Associates"
  - "Why I Stopped Discounting Care Plans"
- **Series**: "Week In The Life of a Practice Owner".

### 2. "Patient Story" (Brez model)
- **Use for**: patient reactions, adjustments, results reveals, conversations with colleagues, podcast or landscape footage, asking the public.
- **Clip**: 15–25 s, the moment plus its payoff, nothing else.
- **Look**: letterboxed landscape on black; lowercase headline above the video that states the setup ("she hadn't turned her neck in 3 years").
- **Captions**: 1–2 words, lowercase, bold rounded sans, colors rotate white → lime → mint, centered on the video.
- **Title**: third person + a CAPS word + 1–2 emoji.
  - "Patient Couldn't Believe What Her X-Ray Showed😳"
  - "Dr. Miller Would NEVER Crack a Neck Like That💀"
  - "-18 Pounds of Inflammation🥀"

### 3. "Clinical Insight" (Jeremy model)
- **Use for**: talking-head teaching, case breakdowns, whiteboard or anatomy explanations, business-of-practice lessons for other clinicians.
- **Clip**: 25–45 s, one principle.
- **Look**: full-bleed talking head, clean dark grade, brand-color keyword punch.
- **Captions**: small white lowercase single words at mid-frame, plus a big green ALL-CAPS keyword on the stressed word every 2–4 s.
- **Post caption**: principle → mechanism or case with numbers → reframe → keyword CTA. For example:

  > Adjusting a patient who skips their home exercises is renting relief, not selling results. The spine you fixed on Tuesday is the same posture on Friday if nothing changed at home. Care that lasts is care the patient does.
  >
  > Comment "PLAN" below and I'll send you my 3-exercise low back routine 👇

- **Idea buckets**: posture and desk life, sleep, back and neck pain myths, sports recovery, why patients quit care, pricing and care plans (for clinicians), the practice-owner mindset.

### "Brains": turning one video into many ideas
For each clip the analysis should also output:
1. The **concrete anchor** in the footage: a patient moment, a number, a tool, an object.
2. The **principle** it proves.
3. **Two to three spin-off ideas** that take the same principle somewhere else, the way Jeremy bridges a game map to business. Example: "the patient who only comes when it hurts" → "the gym member who only goes in January" → "the business owner who only markets when sales drop".
4. A **comment keyword** and a **resource** that fits (PLAN, POSTURE, SLEEP, ROUTINE, SCALE for clinician-facing).

## Implementation spec

What exists: `lib/compose.js` `STYLES` (podcast, talk, bold, minimal) with design fields `font, uppercase, textColor, accentColor, emphasisColor, captionScale, captionY, maxWords, outline, highlight, titleStyle, showTitle, zoom, grade, jumpCuts, leak`. Bundled fonts: Anton, Inter 600/800/900. macOS fonts available to renders: SF Rounded, Avenir Next, DIN Condensed Bold, Impact, Arial Rounded Bold.

| Preset | Fits today | Needs new |
|---|---|---|
| `practiceVlog` (Dom) | `layout: cover`, `font: interBold`, `uppercase: false`, white, `maxWords: 2`, `captionY: 60`, `outline: false`, `highlight: none`, `showTitle: false`, `zoom: false`, `progress: false` | soft drop shadow only (no stroke); moody film grade |
| `patientStory` (Brez) | `maxWords: 2`, lowercase, `outline: false` | **letterbox layout** (16:9 video centered on black, no blurred backdrop); **headline band** above the video (lowercase, rounded bold, 1–2 lines, whole clip); **caption color rotation** per phrase `["#FFFFFF", "#E6F56B", "#86F2D2"]`; rounded font (SF Rounded / Arial Rounded Bold) |
| `clinicalInsight` (Jeremy) | `layout: cover`, small white lowercase captions `maxWords: 1`, `captionY: 50`, dark clean grade | **keyword punch layer**: the clip's emphasis word in `anton` ALL CAPS, brand green `#3DDC5A`, ≈2× caption size, dark shadow, at ≈62%, punching in for ~0.8 s every 2–4 s |

Durations for clip picking: `practiceVlog` 20–35 s, `patientStory` 15–25 s, `clinicalInsight` 25–45 s.

**Titles** (TITLES session, `titles/`): per-preset voice.
- `practiceVlog`: Title Case, 4–8 words, no emoji; lesson / number confession / behind-the-scenes.
- `patientStory`: third person, one CAPS word, 1–2 trailing emoji, curiosity or reaction; plus a lowercase headline, 5–9 words, that states the setup.
- `clinicalInsight`: principle-first post caption (principle → mechanism/case → reframe → keyword CTA); the YouTube title is the principle in under 70 characters.

**Analysis** (`lib/analyze.js`): per clip, add `anchor`, `principle`, `spinoffs[]` (2–3), `commentKeyword`, `resource`, and a `preset` suggestion (story moments with other people → patientStory; to-camera teaching → clinicalInsight; lifestyle/behind-the-scenes → practiceVlog).
