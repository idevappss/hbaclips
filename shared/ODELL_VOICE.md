# Dr. Odell Miller — Voice & Story Bank
Compiled September 22, 2026 from public podcast transcripts, athletics records, and platform bios. Everything here is either (a) said by Odell in his own words on a public podcast, (b) a published record, or (c) a platform bio.

## 1. Timeline
- 1996 born Kalamazoo MI, raised Battle Creek. Gull Lake HS, three-sport athlete, First-Team All-SMAC linebacker, BCAM All-State basketball. Wanted college basketball; "too small at 6'4"".
- 2014 walked on at Western Michigan under P.J. Fleck as a D-end after a highlight tape made a week before signing day. Redshirted.
- 2015 moved to tight end, special teams, no scholarship. Walk-ons didn't eat with the team unless travelling.
- 2016 earned a full scholarship in fall camp, played all 14 games, Academic All-MAC.
- 2017–18 Academic All-MAC; senior year started 11 of 13 at TE.
- Apr 2019 Cleveland Browns mini camp as an undrafted free agent (moved to O-line); also tried out for Washington.
- 2019–20 between NFL and PT school: Uber, a scooter business with his own app (met the mayor; killed by compliance/COVID), Amazon, dropshipping, wedding DJ/videography started with a scholarship check.
- 2020–23 DPT at Midwestern University. Built the Off The Bench training app in years 1–2; sold the list before the app existed. $10K then $12K/month as a student.
- Late 2022 car totalled by a red-light runner; bought a VW van rather than pay six months of Chicago rent while opening a clinic in Michigan, wrote it off through the DJ business.
- Apr/May 2023 graduated, boards, five-week solo van trip to San Diego walking the beach asking "what do I need to do to get there".
- Aug 2023 joined Aaron LeBauer's mastermind *before* opening.
- Fall 2023 saw patients with boxes still on the floor.
- Jan 2024 Off The Bench PT & Sports Performance opens (Kalamazoo/Portage). First full month $33K. Paid ~4 years of rent in the first couple of months. $51K month within six.
- 2024 meets Abby (she DM'd; he didn't reply for 3–4 weeks; a mutual restaurant-owner friend connected them on business).
- Nov 2024 Dr. Nick Miller becomes Clinic Director at OTB.
- 2025 book *The Ultimate Cash Based Practice*; HBA Skool grows; podcast run.
- 2026 living in Miami, HBA at scale.

Public numbers, not yet reconciled: Skool $12.4K/month as a student → $51K/month after graduation; Instagram bio $346k/month; TikTok bio $650k+/month; YouTube banner "Day in the Life of a $1M Physical Therapy business owner".

## 2. Story bank
- **The walk-on.** Nobody D1 wanted him. "I'm actually glad I was a walk-on because I earned my scholarship." Earning it beats being handed it.
- **The NFL removed his fear.** A Browns locker room with Baker Mayfield; after the 0.01% thing nothing in business scared him. Classmates cried before PT practicals; he'd "played in front of a hundred thousand people."
- **Entrepreneur before doctor.** "I'm an entrepreneur turned doctor of physical therapy." Uber after winning games. Speed over perfection: "you only need one thing to work."
- **The app that paid for school.** Posted workouts, 15 people asked for programs, collected a list, sold it, *then* built the app. "My audience decided what they wanted."
- **The van.** Not hardship: cut the expense, keep the vision. Five weeks solo in San Diego: "Why not me?" "Everything happens for you, not to you."
- **Opening the clinic.** Loose business plan so he wouldn't lose the original vision; mastermind before opening; boxes on the floor; $33K first month.
- **Meeting Abby.** She DM'd, he was heads-down for weeks; she owned two chiro offices, a restaurant, real estate. "We're very, very similar."
- **Documenting, not creating.** "I don't create content. I document my life." Seven months of footage banked; Google folders by patient/session for his editor.
- **Anti-standardization.** Asked "why do we do it this way" in PT school; professors often didn't know. "Don't try to standardize me."
- **Money vs. mission.** Clinicians are taught business is dirty and patients pay for it. Money is a tool; "I serve an abundant God"; if it were about money he'd have quit.
- **Speaking / building leaders.** "I used to hate speaking, then something switched." 2026 theme: building leaders beats managing people; the implementation ladder.
- **The mentor lens.** Learned business by osmosis — Gary V on Uber drives, podcasts on 4am workouts. Virtual mentors count.

How he talks: stacked repetition ("do it do it do it"); short declaratives then a "so" that lands the lesson; self-aware about his gaps; vocabulary — zealous, extremist, asset, osmosis, main thing, elevate, speed, document, impact; football framing (reps, game day, preparation, locker room, coach); calls his audience "Doc"; faith stated plainly, never preached.

What he is not: not a hype guy (certainty, not exclamation marks); not a victim narrator (hardship gets reframed); never vague — he gives the number, the month, the name.
