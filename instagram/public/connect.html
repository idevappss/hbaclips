How do I get a token?
  1. In the Instagram app, switch the account to a Business or Creator account (Settings → Account type and tools).
  2. At developers.facebook.com/apps, create an app of type Business and add the Instagram product.
  3. Open Instagram → API setup with Instagram business login, add your account, and click Generate token. If Meta asks, add the account as a tester under App roles and accept the invite in Instagram.
  4. Paste the token above. Repeat for each page you post to.

You don't need Meta's App Review for accounts you own or manage. No Facebook Page is required.
